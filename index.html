QTI ⇄to and from Word Studio

Convert Canvas QTI packages into editable Word documents—and turn a structured Word document back into a Canvas-ready QTI package.

Your files stay on this device. Conversion happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Direction 1

Convert a QTI package to Word

.zip → .docx

Before you begin

  1. Export the quiz from Canvas as a QTI ZIP package. Do not unzip it.
  2. Upload the ZIP below and review the question preview and any warnings.
  3. Download the Word document and verify the question text, answer choices, correct answers, points, and feedback.

Limitations: Version 1 supports multiple choice, multiple answer, True/False, essay, short answer, and text-only directions. Question-bank references, randomized groups, unsupported question types, embedded media, and complex mathematics may require manual review or may not convert.

Direction 2

Convert a structured Word document to QTI

.docx → .zip

Before you begin

  1. Download and use the provided Word template. Keep the bracketed [[QUESTION]] markers and field labels intact.
  2. Mark correct choices with an asterisk, such as *b. Mitochondrion.
  3. Upload the completed DOCX and correct every validation error before downloading the QTI package.
  4. Import the ZIP into an unpublished Canvas sandbox quiz and verify every question in Build and Student Preview before publishing.

Limitations: The converter reads only the supported template structure; it does not interpret arbitrary Word documents. Images, equations, tables inside question stems, question groups, outcomes, bank-selection rules, and advanced New Quizzes question types are not yet generated.

Conversion safeguards and limitations

This tool reduces repetitive quiz entry; it does not replace instructor review. A successful download or Canvas import does not prove that every question was interpreted correctly.

Supported

  • Multiple choice
  • Multiple answer
  • True/False
  • Essay
  • Short answer
  • Text-only directions
  • Points and general feedback

Flagged for review

  • Question groups and bank references
  • Unsupported Canvas question types
  • Missing answer keys
  • Malformed template markers
  • Embedded media and complex mathematics
